About Chrome Tab Groups

TabHarmony interfaces directly with Chrome's native Tab Groups API.

  • Groups created here are standard Chrome tab groups and sync with your active Chrome window.
  • You can right-click any group header in your Chrome browser bar to save, rename, or collapse groups.
  • Tabs inside collapsed groups will take up less space in your browser.
